# Problem Statement

## What Is the Problem?

FMCG distributors must balance **product availability, inventory costs, and fluctuating demand**. Poor demand planning and replenishment decisions can lead to **stockouts, lost sales, excess inventory, and inefficient use of working capital**.

## Who Faces the Problem?

**FMCG distributors and wholesalers in Cameroon**, particularly those managing multiple products, suppliers, and delivery schedules.

## Why Should the Problem Be Solved?

Better inventory decisions can help reduce **unmet demand, unnecessary stock accumulation, and avoidable losses**, while improving **order fulfilment, inventory efficiency, and product availability**.

## Who Benefits From the Solution?

- **Distributors and wholesalers:** improved inventory planning and replenishment decisions.
- **Retailers and customers:** more reliable product availability.
- **Supply-chain managers:** better visibility into inventory performance and operational trade-offs.

## Proposed Solution

Develop a **Cameroon-focused FMCG demand and inventory intelligence platform** that combines public market data, simulated distribution operations, and data analysis to identify inventory-performance patterns and compare replenishment policies under varying demand and delivery conditions.

## Scope and Limitations

The project focuses on **demand, inventory levels, replenishment, delivery lead times, stockouts, and lost sales**. Public data provide market context, while simulated records represent hypothetical company operations. Findings will depend on documented assumptions and will not establish actual company performance or prove real-world improvements without validation against business data.
